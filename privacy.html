Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Truezi Labs LLC ("Truezi Labs," "we," "us") handles information when you visit truezilabs.com (the "Site") or use apps we publish (the "Apps"). Some Apps may have their own privacy notice with more detail; where they do, that notice applies along with this one.

Information we collect

  • This website. The Site has no accounts, forms, advertising or analytics trackers. Our hosting provider may automatically keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and reliability.
  • Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.
  • Our Apps. An App collects only the information it needs to provide its features, as described inside the App and on its App Store listing. Where possible, data stays on your device.

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and improve the Site and Apps
  • To respond to your questions and support requests
  • To protect the security of our services and comply with the law

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us run the Site and Apps (such as hosting or app platform providers), when required by law, or to protect our rights and the safety of our users.

Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, or as required by law, and then delete it.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.
